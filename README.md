🚨 CivixFix AI

AI-Powered Citizen Complaint Management System

🌐 Live Website: https://dharaneesh-sr-07.github.io/Civic-Fix-AI/

---

📌 About the Project

CivixFix AI is an AI-powered citizen complaint management system that helps people report civic problems easily and helps authorities manage and solve those complaints efficiently.

Citizens can submit complaints such as:

- 🕳️ Road potholes
- 💡 Street light problems
- 🗑️ Garbage and waste issues
- 🚰 Water supply problems
- 🚧 Damaged public infrastructure
- 🌳 Other local civic problems

The system uses AI to understand the complaint, identify the problem category, and route it to the appropriate department.

---

🎯 Problem

Citizens often face difficulties when reporting local problems. Complaints may be sent to the wrong department, take too long to process, or have no proper tracking system.

CivixFix AI aims to make this process faster, smarter, and more transparent.

---

💡 Our Solution

CivixFix AI provides a simple process:

Citizen Reports → AI Understands → Department Assigned → Complaint Tracked → Problem Resolved

The system helps reduce manual work and improves communication between citizens and authorities.

---

🔄 How It Works

1. Citizen opens the CivixFix AI website.
2. Citizen submits a complaint.
3. AI analyzes the complaint.
4. The complaint is categorized automatically.
5. It is assigned to the correct department.
6. Authorities update the complaint status.
7. Citizens can track the progress.
8. Delayed complaints can be escalated automatically.
